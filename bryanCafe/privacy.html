Privacy Policy

Welcome to Bryan's Café. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out forms on our website, such as your name, email address, and the contents of any messages you send to us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, improve our services, and manage your interactions with Bryan's Café. We do not sell or share your personal data with any third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risks, protecting your personal data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, such as maintaining preferences and tracking performance. You can choose to set your web browser to refuse cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent.

Links to Third-Party Sites

Our website may contain links to other websites (such as Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of these third-party sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy or data processing practices, please contact us via our Contact form.
